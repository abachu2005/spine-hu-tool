Spine HU Tool

Reproducible trabecular vertebral-HU measurement from CT. Runs fully on your computer — segmentation works offline with no extra setup.

App won't open ("unidentified developer")?

Right-click the app in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. Or run:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Spine HU Tool.app"
